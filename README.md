## Random Bible

Random Bible verses and a Bible verse of the day for your church website, your browser and your
phone. Every verse comes from a public-domain translation: KJV, WEB or ASV.

Paste this snippet into any page. Every visitor sees the same verse that day, and a new one arrives
at midnight.

```html
<div class="random-bible-verse" data-style="window" data-theme="auto">
  <a slot="credit" href="https://random-bible.com/">random-bible.com</a>
</div>
<script src="https://random-bible.com/widget.js" async></script>
```

Pick from six styles, from a photo card to a corner button, and match your site's colours and
font. Your theme's CSS can't break it.
